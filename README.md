# Auroa for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

Auroa is a free media app with profiles, themes and support for the accounts you already have. One app for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

Website: https://auroa.uk
Support: support@auroa.uk

## What you get

- **Profiles** with separate lists and progress for everyone, and an optional PIN.
- **Themes:** six built in, plus a full custom designer for VIP.
- **Continue watching:** pick up where you stopped, and the next episode can start on its own.
- **Trakt and Simkl** to sync what you have watched and your watchlist.
- **Your own accounts:** Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, TorBox and Easynews.
- **Subtitles** and trailers that play as you browse.
- **Offline downloads** on iPhone and iPad, free for everyone. VIP downloads are faster.
- **iPhone and iPad:** laid out for touch, and it turns to landscape.

## Install

Auroa is not on the App Store. You sign the file with your own Apple ID and install it with a sideloading tool such as [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/) (Windows and Mac). The full guide, with steps for each tool, is at https://auroa.uk/tools.html.

### Apple TV

1. Download the Apple TV `.ipa`.
2. On the Apple TV, open Settings, Remotes and Devices, Remote App and Devices, and leave that screen open. Your computer and Apple TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Open your sideloading tool, choose your Apple TV, add the `.ipa`, enter your Apple ID and start.
4. Open Auroa and create a profile.

### iPhone and iPad

1. Download the iPhone and iPad `.ipa`.
2. Connect your device to the computer with a USB cable and tap **Trust** on it.
3. Turn on **Developer Mode**: Settings, Privacy and Security, Developer Mode. The device restarts and asks you to confirm.
4. Open your sideloading tool, add the `.ipa`, enter your Apple ID and start.
5. On the device, open Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, tap your Apple ID email and choose **Trust**.
6. Open Auroa. If it is not on the Home Screen, restart the device.

## Good to know

- A free Apple ID signs an app for **7 days**, so you install it again each week with the same Apple ID. A paid developer account lasts a year.
- A free Apple ID can have **3 sideloaded apps** on an iPhone or iPad at once.
- **Updating:** download the new `.ipa` and install it the same way. It replaces the old copy. The changelog is at https://auroa.uk/changelog.html.
- Use a spare Apple ID if you can.
- Stuck? See the problems list at https://auroa.uk/tools.html#help, or ask in the Discord.
